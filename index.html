A LITTLE GUIDE

How to tidy up your document.

Input is processed as you type. Supported: Markdown tables, tab-separated data copied from a spreadsheet, and plain numbers separated by spaces. Type 0.050 0.048, choose "Right", and you get -4.00%. To compare above and below, put the numbers on two lines.

01

Gain/loss colors

+120k, +30w, +12 turn green; -18k, -10w, -8 turn red. Calculated positive and negative percentages are colored too. Percentages without a sign in your text keep their color. In "down 5pp" and "up 2pp" the number follows the direction word right before it; an explicit sign wins, and a bare 5pp stays uncolored.

02

The direction sets the baseline

Above: (upper − lower) ÷ lower. Below: (lower − upper) ÷ upper. Left: (left − right) ÷ right. Right: (right − left) ÷ left. With several rows or columns, each adjacent pair is compared.

Share: chosen value ÷ (upper + lower). Divide: value ÷ divisor. A zero denominator shows "—".

03

Directives: several tables in one pass

Write "vs above", "vs below", "vs left", "vs right", "upper share", "lower share" or "divide by 7" in a cell to override the sidebar for that block. A blank row starts a new group. With directive detection off, the sidebar settings apply everywhere. The Chinese directives (看上方, 除以 7 …) also work.

04

Take the result to your document

In Preview, "Copy result" copies table formatting and colors for rich-text editors. In Markdown view it copies the source with color spans. The exported .md file keeps the colors as well; how they look depends on whether the target editor renders HTML.

05

Chinese/English punctuation

Pick a direction under "Output → Punctuation". Commas, periods, semicolons, colons, question and exclamation marks, brackets and paired quotes are converted. Decimals, thousands separators, URLs, emails and code are left alone.
